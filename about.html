A bit more
about me.

I was born in the UK but grew up mostly abroad — primary school in Hong Kong, secondary school in Singapore. Moving between cultures at a young age gave me an adaptability I've leaned on ever since, whether adjusting to a new city or switching between the technical and commercial sides of a conversation.

My family now lives in Bath, which means going home involves watching Bath Rugby at the Rec whenever I can. Finn Russell is the best fly-half in the world and I will not be taking questions.

After university I went into enterprise tech sales — first at Darktrace selling AI cybersecurity, then at Panopto selling video management software. I got good at understanding complex technical products and explaining them to people who had twenty minutes and a lot of other priorities. I hit 185% of quota as an AE and generated $3M+ in pipeline as SDR, but the more I learned about the technology I was selling, the more I wanted to build with it myself.

That's what brought me to Northeastern's MS in Computer Science — 4.0 GPA, studying ML, deep learning, and distributed systems. I've built RAG pipelines, a fault-tolerant Kanban board in C++, neural network architectures from scratch in PyTorch, and an ongoing project to generate dance music with MIDI models.

Outside of CS: I boulder at Rockspot most weeks (working on V5s), I cook most nights and have a soft spot for Asian cuisine and anything that takes longer than it should, and I try to travel somewhere new every few months. Recent trips: Colombia, Morocco, Norway.
