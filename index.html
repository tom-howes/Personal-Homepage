a bit about me

London → HK → Singapore → Boston

Growing up across three countries gave me an unusual mix of perspectives. I spent my childhood in Hong Kong and Singapore before moving back to the UK, where my family now lives in Bath.

I spent four and a half years in enterprise tech sales at Darktrace and Panopto before deciding I wanted to understand the technology I was selling from the inside out. That led me to Northeastern's MS in Computer Science — studying ML, distributed systems, and deep learning.

Outside of CS I boulder at Rockspot most weeks, cook ambitious things on weeknights, and go back to the UK whenever I can to watch Bath Rugby at the Rec.
